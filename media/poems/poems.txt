HOW TO ADD A POEM
-----------------
Everything above the first "@title:" line is ignored, so these notes stay private.

To publish a new poem, copy a block below and change the words:

    @title: Your poem title
    @date: ජනවාරි 28, 2026
    @art: your-picture.jpeg
    First line of the poem.
    Second line.

    A blank line makes a stanza break – it is kept exactly as you type it.

Rules:
  - "@title:" starts a new poem (this is required).
  - "@art:" is the picture the poem is read against. Put the file in
    media/poems/art/ and name it here – a bare name is looked for in that
    folder, and anything with a slash in it is used as written. Leave it out
    and the poem falls back to the sleeve of its song, or to one of the
    sleeves in turn if it never became one.
  - "@track:" is optional – name a song and the page gets a play button.
  - "@date:" sets the order. Poetry reads oldest first, so where a poem sits
    in this file does not matter – put new ones anywhere. Sinhala or English
    month names both work, and so does a plain 2026/08/16. Undated poems go last.
  - Save the file, commit, and it appears on the Poetry page automatically.

A note on the picture. It fills a whole screen behind the words, so it wants
to be large – but it is also the first thing the page waits for, so it wants
to be small. Somewhere under two megabytes is the honest middle. The song
sleeves are the exception: those exist twice, and the page paints the little
one first and the sharp one over it, which is why they can be as big as they
are.

That document is the source; this one is what the site reads.
