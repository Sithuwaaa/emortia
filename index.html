About

Producer and sound designer by night, telecommunications engineer by day – Emortia never quite picked a lane. Half the week is spent writing music; the other half is spent on towers, links and BBUs across the Airtel, Dialog and SLT-Mobitel networks.

Eight-plus years in telecom operations, maintenance and project delivery, commissioning multi-vendor BBU and microwave systems. Currently Assistant Project Manager on the Dialog Lamp Pole Project, where the engineering sits alongside coordinating field teams, progress reporting and stakeholder communication.

This site is both halves in one place: a player for the work, and a workspace for everything around it. Built warm, kept honest.
